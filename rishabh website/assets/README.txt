ADD A SONG HERE (OPTIONAL)
==========================

Drop one file named exactly:

  music.mp3

That's it. The floating 🎵 button in the bottom-left appears once the file
loads, and if it isn't here, the button simply never shows up — nothing breaks.

The music NEVER autoplays. The guest has to tap the button, and their choice
is remembered for the rest of the browsing session.

NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Keep it under ~3 MB. A 5-minute lo-fi loop at 128 kbps is roughly 4-5 MB.
- Volume is set to 0.45 in CONFIG.music.volume (top of script.js).
- Using a different filename or format (.m4a, .ogg)? Update CONFIG.music.src.
- Audio with a hard beat fights the tone of the site. Something quiet and
  instrumental works far better than a party anthem.
- Licensing: don't ship a commercial track you don't have the rights to.
